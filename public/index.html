Step up to a beautifully curated material buffet filled with fabrics, buttons, ribbons and reclaimed treasures. Choose what catches your eye, experiment with colour and texture, and turn what you find into a personal detail for your clothing, bag or accessories.

Think of it as a material library and a styling installation in one, a textile treasure hunt where every dish on the table is an invitation. Come with an idea in mind, or come with none at all.
Pick what speaks to you, or let one of our prompts guide you. You'll leave with a one-of-one piece made by your own hands, in a space designed to slow you down and reconnect you with the material and the making.
